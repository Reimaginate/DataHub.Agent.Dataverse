# Support

GitHub Issues are structured intake for reproducible bug reports and feature
requests. They are not a product support channel, do not carry a response SLA,
and do not guarantee that a request will be reviewed or implemented.

Contact [support@reimaginate.online](mailto:support@reimaginate.online) for
product support, configuration assistance, and defect triage.

The MIT License permits modified and redistributed versions of this software.
Reimaginate supports only official, unmodified builds and releases unless
otherwise agreed under a commercial support arrangement. When a report
involves a modified build, Reimaginate may require reproduction against an
official release before providing defect triage.

External pull requests are not accepted. Reproduction details or proposed
patches may be sent through the support channel. Reimaginate decides whether to
independently incorporate suggested changes.
